Tiny Shakespeare

The text is about 40,000 lines from plays by William Shakespeare (1564–1616). Those plays are in
the public domain.

This selection was prepared by Andrej Karpathy as data/tinyshakespeare/input.txt for char-rnn
(https://github.com/karpathy/char-rnn, 2015). The same text is published as the Hugging Face
dataset karpathy/tiny_shakespeare, whose card gives no license and asks for the citation below.
