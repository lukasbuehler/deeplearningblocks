What you’ll build

Recognize a digit

Flatten an MNIST image into numbers. Use a Dense layer and Softmax to turn those numbers into ten digit probabilities.

Sort clothes

Try a hidden layer with Sigmoid or Tanh on Fashion-MNIST. Can your network tell a sneaker from a shirt?

Go deeper

Stack more layers with ReLU. If you train your builds, use the experiment notebook to compare how they do on held-out images.

Check your build to unlock the next lesson. Training is optional and runs in your browser. In Sandbox, all blocks are available from the start.
