What do these settings mean?

Adam adjusts the weights to reduce mistakes. The learning rate sets how big its adjustments are. A batch is the group of examples used for one update. A pass (epoch) goes through all training examples; a step updates the weights once.

Loss measures how wrong the predictions are. Lower is better on the examples being measured. Held-out examples are kept out of weight updates.
